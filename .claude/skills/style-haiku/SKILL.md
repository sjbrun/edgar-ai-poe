# Style: Haiku

<!-- This is a filled-in example of a style knowledge skill. Copy templates/style-skill/ to make new ones. -->

## Hard rules
- Three lines with a 5 / 7 / 5 syllable pattern (English convention)
- No rhyme required

## Conventions
- A *kigo* (season word) anchors the poem in time
- A *kireji* (cutting word), or a break such as a dash, divides two juxtaposed images
- Concrete present-moment imagery, with little abstraction or explanation

## Tone & vocabulary
Quiet, observational, and plain. Nature and everyday moments.

# The Fellow from Crewe

There once was a fellow from Crewe
Who walked into town for a shoe.
His wallet? Not there—
It was home on a chair—
So he trudged home, and his old pair wore through.

---
**Notes:** AABBA in anapestic meter, with three beats in the long lines and two in the short ones. Line 1 uses the classic "There once was a fellow from…" opening, and Crewe gives an easy rhyme. Line 5 uses a fresh rhyme instead of repeating line 1's end word, in the modern style. The twist is that walking home for the wallet wears out the shoes he already had, so now he really does need new ones.

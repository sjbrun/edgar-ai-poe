# The Serpent Grounds

[Verse 1]
'Twas down on the docks of Liverpool town (Liverpool town)
With never a penny to me name (to me name)
A captain came talking of serpents and gold (serpents and gold)
Says, "Sign, lad, you'll never be poor again!" (poor again)

[Chorus]
So heave away, me bully boys (heave away!)
There's gold beneath the serpent's hide (way, hey!)
We're bound across the western ocean
To ride the black and rolling tide!

[Verse 2]
He said, "There's a beast in the western sea (western sea)
With scales like a king's gold crown (king's gold crown)
And a man with a lance and a bit of luck (bit of luck)
Can come home with a thousand pound" (thousand pound)

[Chorus]

[Verse 3]
So we shipped on the Kestrel, forty strong (forty strong)
And we cheered as she cleared the bar (cleared the bar)
With the old man drunk on the quarterdeck (quarterdeck)
And his eye on the western star (western star)

[Chorus]

[Verse 4]
For a month we combed the serpent grounds (serpent grounds)
Till the lookout gave a cry (gave a cry)
And it rose like a church spire out of the deep (out of the deep)
With the lamp of a yellow eye (yellow eye)

[Chorus]

[Verse 5]
We stuck it with irons and lances three (lances three)
And it dragged us a night and a day (night and a day)
Then we hacked off its scales by the barrel-load (barrel-load)
And we reckoned we'd soon draw our pay (draw our pay)

[Chorus]

[Verse 6]
But the old man said, "There's a bigger one yet (bigger one yet)
Where the northern waters freeze (waters freeze)
Ten more and we'll all ride home like lords!" (home like lords)
And he steered for the Greenland seas (Greenland seas)

[Chorus]

[Verse 7]
Then the ice came down and it gripped her fast (gripped her fast)
And the sun went under for good (under for good)
And the cold crept in through the oakum and tar (oakum and tar)
Till it froze through the bones and the wood (bones and the wood)

[Chorus]

[Verse 8]
We burned the spars and we burned the scales (burned the scales)
Of the serpent that made us rich (made us rich)
And we put our mates over, one by one (one by one)
To the ice and the endless pitch (endless pitch)

[Chorus]

[Verse 9]
Now I'm the last of the Kestrel's crew
And there's none left to sing the reply
I've a hold full of gold in the dark and the cold
And I'll lie here alone till I die

[Chorus, slow, one voice]

---
**Notes:** A capstan shanty with crew echoes and a fixed four-line chorus. Verses rhyme ABCB in four-beat lines. It follows the sailor's cycle from the style guide (broke on the docks, lured by a captain, greed, ruin), but there's no trip home at the end. In verse 9 the echoes stop because the crew is gone, and the unchanged chorus is sung by one voice. The cheerful "heave away" becomes a bitter joke.
